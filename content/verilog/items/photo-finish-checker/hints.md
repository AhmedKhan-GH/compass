## 1. Inspect the frame after the camera has updated

Waiting for the edge resumes the checker before the camera's nonblocking update is guaranteed to have happened. With the supplied timescale, a `#1` delay fits inside the observation window. Case equality produces a definite true/false result, but also check that the observed word contains no unknown bits: a reduction XOR becomes unknown if any bit is unknown.

## 2. Present the next runner between shutter edges

The driver owns `bib`; the fixture does not drive it during driver tests. Wait for the chosen clock transition before using a blocking assignment to present the new value. Do not add a delay after that assignment: callers use the task's return to know when the value was presented.
