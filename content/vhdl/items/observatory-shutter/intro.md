`std_logic` has values beyond zero and one. A process can accept only a definite `'1'` request. When a selection control is uncertain, state the policy explicitly in a `case`: choose the manual word for `'1'`, the scheduled word for `'0'`, and preserve only bit positions where both candidates agree otherwise. Equality on `std_logic_vector` compares its enumeration values literally, so matching `X` or `Z` positions compare equal; use `is_x` separately when testing validity.
